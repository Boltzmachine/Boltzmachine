## About me
🎓 I am a fourth-year computer science Ph.D. student at Yale University, advised by Prof. [Rex Ying](https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=6fqNXooAAAAJ&hl=en). I previously obtained my B.S. degree at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. [Jake Zhao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8ipao8MAAAAJ&hl=en).

🪐 My research lies at the intersection of multimodal large language models and neuroscience. I develop unified architectures that extend LLMs beyond text to reason over images, video, time series, actions, and neural signals for brain–computer interfaces and robotic systems.

🔧 In addition to my academic pursuits, I actively contribute to several open-source projects, such as [Blender](https://projects.blender.org/Weikang-Qiu?tab=activity).
